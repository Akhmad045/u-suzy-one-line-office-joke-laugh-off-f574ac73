Entry for the make.fun bounty [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73).

The line, all 108 characters of it:

> I joined the company for the free coffee; three years later, the coffee is still free and so is my overtime.

Why this line: it starts on the most ordinary office perk there is —
free coffee, the thing everyone actually joins for — and then the word
"free" quietly changes sides. Three years later the coffee is still
free, and so is the overtime: the perk never changed, the price did.
One concrete detail, one word doing double duty, and the punchline
lands in the last four words with nothing left to explain.

## How to run it

```sh
python3 main.py                        # writes the line to /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test            # checks the line against the 200-char gate
python3 main.py --out /tmp/entry.txt   # write somewhere else
```

`main.py` is standard library only. It writes the line exactly as-is,
with no trailing newline, and refuses to write anything that breaks the
gate (empty, over 200 characters, more than one line, or carrying a
control character).
